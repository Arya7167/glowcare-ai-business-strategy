# GlowCare – AI Business Strategy & Adoption

## Project Overview
A 7-day beginner-level AI Business Strategy case study focused on evaluating AI adoption opportunities for a fictional Indian D2C skincare startup.

## Business Scenario
GlowCare receives approximately 1,000 customer queries every month. Its customer support team faces repetitive questions, delayed responses and increasing operational workload.

## Project Objective
Evaluate how AI can improve customer support operations, customer experience and business efficiency.
