# GlowCare – AI Business Strategy & Adoption

**Project:** AI Business Strategy Case Study  
**Industry:** D2C Skincare / E-commerce  
**Role:** AI Business Strategy Consultant  
**Duration:** 7 Days  
**Company:** Fictional Indian Startup

---

# Project Overview

GlowCare is a fictional Indian direct-to-consumer skincare startup. This project explores how AI can address business challenges, improve customer experience and create measurable business value.

The objective is to evaluate AI adoption from a business perspective rather than simply implementing technology.

---

# Day 1 – Business Problem Identification

## 1. Business Background

GlowCare receives approximately 1,000 customer queries every month.

Its customer support team faces:
- Repetitive customer questions.
- Delayed responses.
- Increasing operational workload.

## 2. Identified Business Problem

Customer support is consuming excessive time and resources due to repetitive customer queries.

## 3. Primary Business Objective

Reduce repetitive customer-support workload while maintaining customer satisfaction.

## 4. Questions for the Founder

1. What budget is available for AI adoption?
2. Does the company have an internal technical team?
3. What is the expected implementation timeline?
4. What are the biggest customer-support challenges?
5. How will success be measured?

## 5. Day 1 Outcome

Identified customer-support inefficiency as the initial business problem requiring investigation.

# Day 2 – Customer & Business Analysis

## 1. Monthly Customer Query Distribution

| Query Category | Monthly Queries |
|---|---:|
| Order tracking | 300 |
| Product FAQs | 250 |
| Delivery questions | 150 |
| Returns and refunds | 150 |
| Product complaints | 100 |
| Other | 50 |
| **Total** | **1,000** |

*All figures are hypothetical assumptions.*

## 2. Initial Automation Opportunity

Order tracking was identified as a potential first automation opportunity because:
- It represents 30% of monthly queries.
- Questions are repetitive.
- Responses can be retrieved from existing order and shipping data.
- It addresses customer uncertainty after purchase.

## 3. Expected Business Benefits

**Customer experience:**
- Reduced uncertainty about orders.
- Greater transparency.
- Improved customer convenience.
- Better confidence in the company.

**Business operations:**
- Reduced repetitive support workload.
- Faster responses.
- Potential improvement in customer retention.

Customer retention is a potential benefit that must be validated through actual repeat-purchase data.

## 4. Success Metrics

1. Automated resolution rate.
2. Average response time.
3. Customer satisfaction (CSAT).
4. Cost per resolved query.
5. Repeat purchase rate.

## 5. Day 2 Outcome

Identified order tracking as a promising automation opportunity, with potential benefits for both operational efficiency and customer experience.
